# Evaluator rubric v1 (example candidate)

The rubric for the fictional candidate in profile.example/. Same structure as
a real profile's rubric: the model assigns ten sub-scores on fixed scales and
reports gating facts; code applies the weights, the gates, the salary floor
and the verdict thresholds. Replace the Background section and the story list
in 5d with your own to use it.

---

## Your job

Assign the ten sub-scores defined below and report what you found for the
gating checks. **Do not compute weighted totals, do not pick the verdict, and
do not do any arithmetic.** Code applies the weights, the gates and the
thresholds. Inventing a total is worse than useless, because it will be
silently discarded and it wastes the reasoning that should have gone into the
sub-scores.

### When the posting does not say

Score only what the posting supports. If a sub-score's input is genuinely
absent from the posting, set `abstained: true` and leave the score null.

**Never substitute a low score for a missing fact.** A posting that omits its
salary is not a posting with a bad salary. Guessing turns silence into
evidence, and the verdict logic is built to handle an honest gap but not a
fabricated figure.

## Background — the candidate

6 yrs Brightpath Mobile (consumer fintech app, 3M users): Data Analyst →
Senior Analyst → Product Manager (Growth), ~2 yrs PM title. MBA (in progress,
graduating 2026); BS Economics. AI/ML: built a recommendation model for in-app
offers, shipped an LLM-based support triage pilot. Metrics: 35% lift in
first-week activation, 18% fewer support tickets, 2x referral sign-ups. US work
authorization. Location requirement: New York (NYC / NY metro) only — New
Jersey and fully remote US roles are acceptable but scored down; other US and
international roles are not being pursued.

Known gaps — major/bridgeable: advertiser-side ad platforms; enterprise B2B
sales motions; hardware. Structural: deep ML research/engineering depth; 7+
yrs PM-title required; declining BU.

Career path: Job 1 = own a measurable growth product at a well-known US tech
company (2–3 yrs). Job 2 = lead a product area with a P&L or growth metric.
The test is "does this build a coherent chapter toward product leadership?",
not "does it maximize PM reps."

## Company tier

Tier reflects one thing only: **does the brand name alone get the candidate
past an ATS/recruiter screen**, independent of role fit. Not prestige, not
referral access.

**Tier 1** — brand alone clears the screen, any tech role:
Google · Meta · Amazon · Microsoft · Apple · Nvidia · Anthropic · OpenAI

**Tier 2** — opens the door, does not clear the screen alone:
Netflix · TikTok · LinkedIn · Uber · Airbnb · DoorDash · Instacart · Spotify ·
Robinhood · Datadog · Salesforce · Databricks · Snowflake · Palantir ·
ServiceNow · Block · Figma · Reddit · Samsung · Sony Interactive · xAI ·
Whatnot · Distyl AI · Decagon · Resolve AI · Harvey · Cognition · Sierra ·
McKinsey Digital / BCG X / Bain Vector-type consulting

**Tier 3** — brand does nothing for ATS; experience alone carries it:
Everything else, including Comcast/NBCU · Starbucks · Nike · Best Buy ·
Walmart · Target · T-Mobile / AT&T · Cohere · Writer · Glean · Tenex

**A company not on any list above is Tier 3.** You cannot search, so you cannot
verify VC backing or valuation, and an unverified claim of Tier 2 is exactly
the error the tier exists to prevent. Say in the rationale that the placement
is an unlisted default rather than a researched one.

## Score 1 — Career aspiration fit

| Sub-score | Scale |
|---|---|
| **4a Function alignment** | PM (Growth/Product/AI/Platform) that ships a product and owns a user-facing metric = 10 · PMM/S&O/Growth Marketing, or embedded Product/AI/Growth analytics with a named owned metric = 8 · Pure DA/BA — **or** Solutions PM/Deployment Strategist/AI Strategist/FDE/CX-AI/GTM-enablement work that advises, configures, or supports without shipping product or owning a user-facing metric, regardless of AI/ML content = 4 · Traditional marketing/brand/events = 3 · Other (eng, ops, finance) = 1 |
| **4b Company tier** | Tier 1 = 10 · Tier 2 = 8 · Tier 3 = 4 |
| **4c Seniority & scope trajectory** | Four checks: (1) ships measurable growth product in year 1, (2) promotion/scope growth in 2–3 yrs, (3) owns a user-facing metric, (4) builds US tech network. All four = 10, −2 per miss. For bridge roles, swap (1) for "owns a named account or product feedback loop with documented PM conversion." |
| **4d Location** | New York (NYC / NY metro) = 10 · New Jersey = 6 · Remote US = 6 · Bay Area / Seattle / LA / other US / international = 0 · **not stated → abstain** |

A Solutions/Deployment/AI-Strategist/FDE/CX-AI title is not an exception to
the 4a scale. Score it on whether it ships product and owns a metric — not on
how much AI it involves.

## Score 2 — JD versus background

| Sub-score | Scale |
|---|---|
| **5a Keyword/signal match** | Extract the JD's top 5–7 capability signals — infer the underlying skill, not the literal keyword. 10 = 5+ evidenced with strong metrics · 7 = 3–4 evidenced, some metrics · 4 = 1–2 weakly evidenced · 1 = none |
| **5b YOE & title framing** | 10 = squarely in the JD's band · 7 = slight mismatch bridged by the MBA · 4 = meaningful gap (no matching title, or 10+ yrs asked for a mid-level role) · 1 = hard mismatch (>3 yrs off, or wrong title family) · **band not stated → abstain** |
| **5c Education & hard requirements** | 10 = MBA preferred/required, work auth fine, all hard reqs met · 7 = no blockers · 4 = missing one preferred qualification · 1 = missing a hard requirement |
| **5d Story depth & relevance** | Do the stories survive probing for THIS role? Available: offer recommendation model (ML 0→1) · first-week activation +35% (growth, XFN) · support triage LLM pilot (AI feature, ops efficiency) · referral program 2x (acquisition) · 3M-user app (consumer scale) · analyst-to-PM promotion (influence, scope growth). 10 = 3+ map with outcomes that survive "tell me more" · 7 = 2 map, quantified · 4 = 1 maps · 1 = none |
| **5e Gap exposure** | 10 = no meaningful gaps · 7 = 1 major · 4 = 2 major, or 1 structural · 1 = 3+ or fundamental mismatch |
| **5f Why-this-company defensibility** | 10 = Tier 1–2 with specific product or narrative conviction · 7 = Tier 1–2, clear generic fit · 4 = Tier 3, plausible but generic · 1 = visibly weak or contradictory |

## Gating checks

Report what the posting says. **Do not decide the verdict from these** — code
applies them. If the posting is silent, report null; silence is not a pass and
not a failure.

- `pm_years_required` — years of **PM-title-specific** experience demanded, not
  total years of experience. Integer, or null if unstated.
- `declining_bu` — is this a structurally declining business unit where owning a
  growth metric in year 1 is impossible? True/false, or null.
- `pure_ic_research` — a pure IC research role with no user-facing ownership and
  no PM path? True/false, or null.
- `clearance_required` — does it require a security clearance? True/false, or null.

The salary floor is checked in code against the stored compensation range and
is not your concern.

---

## Reasoning

Three to five sentences. Lead with what drove Score 1, then what drove Score 2,
then any gap or gating finding. Direct, no hedging, no recommendation — the
verdict is computed, not argued.
